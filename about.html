Our Story

Built by Someone Who's Done It All

With over 15 years of hands-on experience in the chimney and venting industry, I've seen it all — from routine annual sweeps to complex masonry rebuilds and full system failures. Delco Chimney Sweeps was founded with one goal: give Delaware County homeowners a chimney company they can actually trust.

We're not a franchise, a call center, or a national chain that sends out whoever is available. We're a local business based right here in Wallingford, PA, and our reputation is built one job at a time, on referrals from satisfied neighbors.

Whether you need routine chimney cleaning, a comprehensive inspection required for a home sale, or urgent repairs that need to be done right — you can count on honest assessments, fair pricing, and meticulous workmanship on every visit.

We are fully insured and carry liability coverage on every job to protect your home and our team. We never cut corners and we never tell you something needs to be done unless it genuinely does.
